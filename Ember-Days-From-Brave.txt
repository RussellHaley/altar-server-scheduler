In the Personal Ordinariate of the Chair of Saint Peter, the Advent Ember Days are fixed to the Wednesday, Friday, and Saturday of the first week of Advent.  This differs from the traditional Roman calendar, which places them in the week following St.  Lucy’s Day (December 13).

The shift to the first week of Advent was implemented in the Divine Worship missal to preserve the unique liturgical character of the final days of Advent (December 17–24), known as the O Antiphons or Sapientiatide.  Despite the earlier scheduling, Ember Friday in Advent remains an obligatory day of abstinence from meat for all Ordinariate members aged 14 and older. 

For 2025, the Advent Ember Days were December 3, 5, and 6.  For 2026, they will fall on November 25, 27, and 28.
